# About Me

Hi, I'm Qianyun (Devon) Deng! I work at the intersection of **product, AI, and business operations**, turning complex problems into practical digital solutions.

My background is in computational linguistics and Human Language Technology, but my interests have expanded well beyond language models themselves. I'm especially interested in how technology can improve the way people work, access information, and make decisions.

## What I Do

I enjoy working across both the **technical and human sides of building products**: understanding user needs, defining requirements, designing workflows, and bringing solutions into real-world use.

At **Good Samaritan Shelter**, I served as a Product Management Co-lead on a nonprofit website redesign. I contributed to user research, product requirements, and cross-functional coordination, and supported usability testing and client handoff. The redesigned website launched and remains in use.

At **PeopleLens**, I built AI-powered GTM automation using Python, LLM APIs, and CRM integrations. My work focused on transforming messy customer data and manual outreach processes into more structured, scalable workflows.

I also bring experience in **financial services operations at UMB**, where I've worked with operational processes, data accuracy, internal controls, and automation opportunities.

Together, these experiences have shaped how I approach technology: a solution should not only work technically, but also solve a meaningful problem for the people using it.

## What You'll Find Here

My portfolio is organized into two areas:

**[Product & Design](/portfolio/)** — User research, product requirements, UX decisions, and cross-functional product delivery.

**[AI & Automation](/portfolio/)** — AI agents, Python scripting, API integrations, and workflow automation.

I enjoy projects that combine problem-solving, technical implementation, and an understanding of real business needs.

## What I'm Looking For

I'm interested in opportunities across **digital product management, AI solutions, and automation**, particularly where I can work closely with stakeholders, translate complex needs into actionable solutions, and help bring new capabilities into production.

I'm always interested in connecting with people building useful technology and solving interesting problems.
